# Ideas, Hypotheses & Backlog (RFCs)

This document preserves ideas, design proposals, and future hypotheses for **Pillars of Fortune**.

---

## 1. Spectator Experience Enhancements
- **Spectator Compass / Teleporter**: Give spectators an item in their hotbar to open a GUI or cycle through alive players.
- **Speed Selector**: Hotbar item (e.g. Feather) to quickly toggle flight speed between 1x, 2x, and 3x.
- **Auto-Cam Mode**: Automatically switch camera perspective to players in combat or when a disaster strikes near them.

---

## 2. Dynamic Disaster Enhancements
- **Laser Beam Disaster**: Guardian or custom particle beam sweeping across the arena horizontally.
- **Tsunami / Acid Wave**: Rising flood of damaging liquid that forces players to climb or place blocks higher.
- **Low Gravity Moon Phase**: Reduced gravity combined with knockback sticks for mid-air combat.
- **Blindness Fog**: Sudden thick fog limiting vision to 3 blocks while bats or phantoms spawn.

---

## 3. Procedural Map Themes
- **Nether Fortress Theme**: Red nether bricks, basalt pillars, lava center, with blazes and wither skeleton archers.
- **End Void Theme**: Purpur and end stone pillars with chorus fruit teleportation mechanics and endermites.
- **Cyberpunk / Neon City Theme**: Sea lanterns, colored glass, concrete, with fireworks and wind charges.

---

## 4. Player Statistics & Leaderboards
- In-memory session tracking for:
  - Most wins
  - Most eliminations
  - Longest survival time
- End-of-game summary display in chat showing game stats and MVP.
